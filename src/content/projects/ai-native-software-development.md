## What it is

Not a repository. A practical AI-native software development workflow, developed
and refined through the projects on this page and through client work: reusable
skill packs, agent workflows, task-management tooling, and development standards
covering planning, implementation, testing, review, and delivery.

It has been applied to both independent products and professional engineering
engagements.

## Why it has its own page

Because it's the thread. The tools didn't arrive first and get a methodology
bolted on afterwards — the practice kept running into the same gaps, and the
tools are what those gaps turned into.
