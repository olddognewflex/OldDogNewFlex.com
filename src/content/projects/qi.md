## What it is

Qi is a local-first personal productivity and AI tooling platform. It handles
context-aware execution, on-demand AI capabilities, knowledge capture,
automation, and workflow orchestration — a lightweight foundation for folding AI
into everyday development and personal workflows rather than a separate place
you go to use AI.

## Why it exists

Most AI tooling assumes the interesting thing is the model. I think the
interesting thing is the *context* — where your work lives, what you were doing
an hour ago, which project you're in, what you already decided. That context sits
on your machine, and the tools that need it mostly don't have it.

Qi is a bet that AI becomes genuinely useful when it's part of your computing
environment, not another application you visit.

## Current state

Building. It's the central ongoing project here, which also means it's the one
