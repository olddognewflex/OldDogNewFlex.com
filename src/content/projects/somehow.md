## What it is

An iPhone app taken from concept through App Store release — designed,
developed, and shipped. Built with Expo and React Native on an offline-first
architecture that works without an account or a server behind it.

## Why it exists

Because shipping a complete product is a different discipline from building a
technology demo, and I wanted to do the whole thing: product design,
implementation, testing, release. The offline-first, no-account decision wasn't
a technical flourish — it's what the app needed in order to be trustworthy.

## What it proves

AI-assisted SDLC practices, custom development tooling, and structured task
workflows were applied across the entire lifecycle. Somehow is the end of the
pipeline the rest of the work on this page feeds into: the point where a
development practice has to produce something real people install.

## Current state

Shipped and in production.
